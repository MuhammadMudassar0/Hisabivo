HISABIVO: COMPETITIVE REVIEW AND PRODUCT PRIORITIES
Reviewed 25 November 2026

Scope
This is a review of published product pages and official help documentation,
compared with the Hisabivo implementation. Competitor claims were not verified
inside authenticated accounts. An unmentioned feature is not proof of absence.
Recommendations below are product hypotheses to validate with users, not claims
that no competitor provides them.

WHAT COMPETITORS ALREADY COVER

DigiKhata (Pakistan)
Its official features page describes customer/supplier ledgers, a cash book,
collection reminders, PDF sharing, bill attachments, invoicing, multiple languages,
and app locking. Urdu/local language support, Udhar, and PDFs alone are therefore
weak positioning for Hisabivo. Our gaps include receipt attachments, language
support, and customer/supplier workflows.
Source: https://www.digikhata.pk/features

Udhaar Book (Pakistan)
Its Staff Book advertises attendance, overtime, salary calculations, advances,
carried dues, salary slips, and salary transfers. The site links a web version.
Hisabivo's new employee salary register is a foundation, not equivalent payroll
coverage. Attendance, managed salary advances, employee statements, and immutable
period-specific payslip breakdowns are follow-up gaps in our product.
Source: https://udhaar.pk/staff-book/

Wallet by BudgetBakers (international)
Its product pages cover budgeting, bank sync, planned payments, and forecasting.
Those categories are established. Its official Planned Payments help page says
creation is unavailable in the web app, missing calendar dates such as the 31st
are skipped, and multi-device offline generation may duplicate records. These
are documented limitations of that workflow, not of every competing product.
Hisabivo already supports web recurrence, last-day clamping, and server-side
occurrence uniqueness. We should demonstrate and regression-test those specific
behaviors. Hisabivo currently requires connectivity for signed-in data; it does
not yet offer conflict-safe offline synchronization or bank matching.
Sources:
https://budgetbakers.com/en/products/wallet/features/planned-payments/
https://support.budgetbakers.com/hc/en-us/articles/7149523920786-Setup-Planned-Payments

Zoho Expense (international business)
Its documentation covers receipt scanning, categorization, and approval and
reimbursement workflows. Receipt automation is an established capability. Our
gaps include receipt capture, team approvals, reimbursement tracking, and audit
history. We should not market a private individual ledger as enterprise-ready.
Sources:
https://www.zoho.com/us/expense/help/expenses/autoscan-receipts/
https://www.zoho.com/us/expense/expense-faqs/

RECOMMENDED POSITIONING

A clear PKR cash-flow workspace for a Pakistani small-business owner: today's
recorded money, upcoming bills and salaries, expected client receipts, and Udhar
given or taken, with every partial payment traceable.

This is a focused positioning hypothesis. Forecasting, staff management, and
credit ledgers each exist elsewhere. The opportunity is to make their combined
workflow easier to understand and consistently available on the web and phone.
Validate that benefit before making superiority claims.

NEXT PRODUCT PRIORITIES

1. Accounts and transfers, then a transparent cash forecast.
   Add opening balances for cash/bank/Easypaisa/JazzCash. Transfers must not count
   as income or expenses. Show recorded cash, committed payments, and expected
   receipts separately for 7/30/90 days. Example: cash 25,000 minus obligations
   30,000 = a 5,000 gap before any unreceived income. This is planning from entered
   records, not a verified bank balance or a promise of funds.

2. Complete the staff-pay workflow.
   Add salary advances that can be applied once, period-specific pay breakdowns,
   printable employee statements, and optional attendance/overtime. Preserve old
   periods when rates change and keep every payment linked to exactly one bill.
   Manual deductions must remain clearly distinct from statutory payroll.

3. Help users bring and trust their records.
   CSV import/export, import previews and duplicate review, receipt attachments,
   recoverable deletion, an edit audit trail, and a tested backup/restore process.

4. Reduce daily entry work in local language.
   Start with Urdu interface/PDF support and quick reusable entries. Evaluate
   Roman Urdu or voice entry using examples such as "Ali ko 5 hazar advance".
   Always show the parsed person, amount, type, and date for confirmation before
   saving. AI is deferred; deterministic templates can provide value first.

5. Add controlled collaboration when targeting teams.
   Separate personal/business workspaces, owner/accountant/staff permissions,
   approvals, and scoped exports. Current private user accounts are not shared
   organizations. Keep reminder recipients and channels under explicit control.

VALIDATION BEFORE THE NEXT LARGE BUILD

Use task walkthroughs with small-business owners, freelancers, and bookkeepers.
Ask them to record a partial supplier payment, pay part of a salary, collect
Udhar, and find obligations due before their next client receipt. Measure task
completion, entry time, misunderstandings about expected versus actual cash,
and repeat use. Validate willingness to pay for the workflow rather than for
the size of the feature list. No interviews have been conducted for this review.
